3. AI Infrastructure Reality Check

No web crawling: Current AI only parses text manually provided by users. It cannot browse X, Facebook,
YouTube, or Google in real time.

Free model limitations: Current free-tier models have rate limits, no SLA guarantees, inconsistent output
formats, and unreliable structured output.

No validation layer: Hallucinated scores or player names can be written directly into the database.

No retry/fallback logic: Single API failures can break sync operations.

5. Strategic Direction
The immediate priority is stabilizing and structuring the data layer before expanding AI ingestion capabilities or
monetization features. Without strong validation, proper schemas, and automated recalculation systems, scaling data
ingestion will increase inconsistency and technical debt. The focus should therefore remain on: Reliable structured
data models Automated standings and fixture systems Player and event-level tracking Validation and sync reliability
Once the foundation is stable, the platform can evolve into a scalable football data infrastructure product for the NWFL
ecosystem.
